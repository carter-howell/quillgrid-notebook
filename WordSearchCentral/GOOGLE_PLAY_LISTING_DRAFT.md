# Google Play Listing Draft

## App Name

QuillGrid Notebook

## Short Description

Turn your own writing into cozy letter-grid puzzle chapters.

## Full Description

QuillGrid Notebook is a relaxing letter-grid puzzle maker built around your own stories.

Paste a short story, journal-style scene, classroom prompt, or original writing, then let the game turn each sentence into a playable level. Find the selected words on the board, complete the puzzle, and unlock the next line in your notebook.

Features:

- Create custom story collections.
- Generate letter-grid levels from pasted text.
- Tune the number of words per level.
- Use dynamic word selection to focus on stronger story words.
- Keep multiple stories in the Story Manager.
- Play with touch, mouse, or keyboard shortcuts.
- Change fonts and color themes.
- Use hints when you get stuck.
- Save progress locally on your device.

No account is required. Stories and progress are stored locally on your device.
