# QuillGrid Notebook Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

QuillGrid Notebook is a letter-grid puzzle game that lets players create story-based levels from text they enter.

## Information We Collect

QuillGrid Notebook does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Stories, game progress, display settings, and audio settings are saved locally on the player's device so the game can work as expected. This information stays on the device unless the player separately backs up, shares, or transfers their device data through operating-system features outside the game.

## Internet, Accounts, And Analytics

QuillGrid Notebook does not require an account, does not use analytics, does not include advertising, and does not send gameplay or story text to a server.

## Children

QuillGrid Notebook is intended as a general-audience puzzle game. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Deletion

Players can delete locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app from their device.
